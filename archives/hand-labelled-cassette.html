A hand-labelled cassette

A copied tape, a handwritten title, and a song that travelled.

Type
Object
Date

Place

In the 1970s and 1980s, the cassette was how music moved in Morocco. Shops in every market sold blank tapes and copies of the latest recordings, and many listeners made their own.

Handwritten labels like this one are small documents in themselves: a title, sometimes a date or a name, written by whoever made the copy. They tell us what people chose to keep.
