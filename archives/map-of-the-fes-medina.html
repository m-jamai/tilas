A map of the Fès medina

The streets, gates and quarters of the old city on paper.

Fès el-Bali, the old medina, is one of the largest car-free urban areas in the world: a maze of thousands of lanes, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1981.

Old maps help read it. They show which gates were in use, where the markets stood and how the quarters were divided, and they let us compare the city on paper with the city as it is now.
