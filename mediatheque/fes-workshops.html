Fès Workshops

Photographs from the tanneries, metalworkers and weavers of the old city.

Photo series6 photos27 September 2026

About this work

Inside the medina of Fès, crafts are still organised by street and by square: the tanners near the river, the coppersmiths on the Seffarine square, the weavers and the dyers in their own corners.

This series follows a working day across those workshops, from the first light on the tanning pits to the last hammer blow in the metalworkers’ square.
