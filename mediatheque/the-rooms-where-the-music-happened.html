The Rooms Where the Music Happened

Film, interviews and archive photographs from the venues of 1970s Casablanca.

Documentary42 min4 October 2026

About this work

In the 1970s, the new Moroccan groups did not only play on the radio. They played in cinemas turned into concert halls, in youth centres, in wedding tents and on rooftops. Most of these rooms have closed, changed or disappeared.

This film goes back to them. It visits the buildings that remain, listens to the people who were in the audience, and sets their memories against photographs and recordings from the period.
